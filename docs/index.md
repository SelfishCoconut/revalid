# revalid

**AI-Driven System for the Revalidation of Pentest Findings** — Bachelor's Thesis (TFG), ESII-UCLM, by Álvaro Navarro.

`revalid` parses penetration-testing reports, extracts findings and their reproduction steps, and autonomously re-executes them against **authorized lab targets** to verify whether applied fixes are effective.
